Chi-square test was performed to examine the relationship between payment method and customer churn. The test produced a χ² statistic of 648.142 with 3 degrees of freedom and a p-value of approximately 3.68 × 10⁻¹⁴⁰. Since the p-value is below the 0.05 significance level, the null hypothesis of independence was rejected. This indicates a statistically significant association between payment method and churn in the analyzed dataset.
